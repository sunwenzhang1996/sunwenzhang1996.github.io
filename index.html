Structured Spatiotemporal Modeling

Wenzhang Sun 孙文章

I work on structured spatiotemporal modeling for generative and multimodal systems, with a focus on human-centric video generation, efficient 3D/4D world representation, and multimodal evaluation.

My recent work at Li Auto Foundation Models spans digital humans, audio-visual generation, video generation acceleration, compact video latents, 4D video editing, dynamic driving-scene 3DGS, and item-level Video LLM evaluation. Before that, I received my Ph.D. from Beijing Institute of Technology, where I worked on 3D human reconstruction and multi-camera motion analysis.
