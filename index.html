Wenzhang Sun 孙文章

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Li Auto through a joint program with Tsinghua University. My research focuses on video generation, controllable generative models, and compact spatiotemporal representations.

I am particularly interested in advancing these capabilities toward interactive world models and Physical AI.

I received my Ph.D. from Beijing Institute of Technology, where I worked on 3D human modeling and neural reconstruction.
